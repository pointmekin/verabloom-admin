# Record payouts

Type: task
Status: ready-for-agent

## Scope

Record completed transfers from the central account to Chompooh or Kan. Show an authenticated admin table of payout date, recipient, amount, and optional note on the finance page.

## Acceptance criteria

- Payouts persist with exact Thai baht amounts and a calendar date.
- Only Chompooh and Kan are valid payout recipients.
- The finance page can record a payout and lists payouts in its selected reporting period.
- The payout total is deducted from the central-account balance for that period.
- The page has Thai and English labels and works in its narrow-screen table layout.
